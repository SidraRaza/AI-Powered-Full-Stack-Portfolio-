# Quickstart Guide: Update About Section of Portfolio

## Overview
Quick implementation guide to update the About section of the portfolio with information about Sidra Raza as an "Agentic AI Developer from Pakistan" and expertise in Agentic AI and AI automation.

## Pre-Implementation Steps

1. Locate the About page component (likely in `src/app/about/page.tsx` or similar)
2. Identify where the current About content is defined
3. Review the current structure and layout

## Implementation Steps

### 1. Update About Section Content
1. Open the About page component (e.g., `src/app/about/page.tsx`)
2. Replace the current About content with the new content following these guidelines:
   - Write in first person ("I")
   - Create 2-3 short, readable paragraphs
   - Clearly explain who Sidra Raza is, where she's from (Karachi, Pakistan)
   - State professional role as "Agentic AI Developer & AI Ops Builder"
   - Highlight expertise in Agentic AI, AI automation, and business optimization
   - Mention "Word Weaver AI Planner" as an example project
   - Focus on building AI systems that automate businesses and optimize workflows

### 2. Add SEO Keywords Naturally
1. Include these keywords naturally in the content:
   - "Sidra Raza"
   - "Agentic AI Developer from Pakistan"
   - "Sidra Raza Karachi"
   - "AI Automation Expert"
   - "AI Systems Builder"
2. Ensure keywords are incorporated naturally without keyword stuffing

### 3. Update Page Metadata (if needed)
1. If the About page has specific SEO requirements, update the metadata in the page component
2. Ensure the page title and description include relevant keywords

### 4. Verify Content Structure
1. Check that content is written in first person ("I")
2. Confirm content consists of 2-3 readable paragraphs
3. Verify professional, confident, and clear tone
4. Ensure no emojis or technical implementation details are included

## Verification Steps

1. Visit the About page and confirm the new content is displayed
2. Check that content clearly explains who Sidra Raza is, where she's from, and what she does professionally
3. Verify the content includes all required SEO keywords naturally
4. Confirm the content is written in first person with 2-3 readable paragraphs
5. Ensure Sidra Raza is clearly positioned as an "Agentic AI Developer from Pakistan"
6. Verify the content highlights expertise in Agentic AI and AI automation with real-world business value
7. Confirm the "Word Weaver AI Planner" project is mentioned as an example
8. Test responsive design on different screen sizes
9. Inspect page source to confirm proper meta tags and keywords

## Timeline
- Implementation: 15-30 minutes
- Testing: 10-15 minutes
